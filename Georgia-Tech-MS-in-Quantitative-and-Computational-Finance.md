## My notes from the admissions session

These notes summarize the QCF information-session slides I saved. They are intended to give my recommenders context about the program's admissions priorities.

### Academic preparation

- The session placed less emphasis on overall GPA alone and more emphasis on relevant coursework and the story shown by the transcript.
- Quantitative aptitude was identified as an application component.
- The session distinguished between objective and subjective application components.

### Work and internship experience

- **Full-time work experience is not required** for admission.
- Experience can help offset weaker components of an application through holistic review.
- **Internship experience is strongly recommended**, with a preference for experience lasting **three months or longer**.
- The experience does **not** have to be in financial services.

### Statement of purpose and short answers

The session discussed the statement of purpose and short-answer questions as subjective components of the application.

- **Project or research experience:** one question discusses a project or research experience from a class, capstone, or lab and its relevance to the applicant's candidacy.
- **Programming experience:** describe the programming languages you are comfortable using and your proficiency in each. The slide gives a **500-word maximum**.
- **Optional context:** explain discrepancies or weaknesses that would benefit from context, such as employment gaps, low grades, recommendations not coming from a recent supervisor, unusual work experience or career paths, or other challenges.

### Choosing recommenders

- Use **academic or professional references**, rather than personal references.
- Any combination of academic and professional references is acceptable.
- Recommenders do **not** need to have a connection to finance.
- Choose people who can represent you well, rather than choosing solely on the basis of their rank or seniority.

For my recommenders, I hope these notes provide useful context. Please draw on the coursework, projects, working relationships, and qualities you have observed firsthand.

### Interview expectations

The session emphasized:

- Being prepared without sounding memorized.
- Being able to walk through your résumé.
- Demonstrating professionalism and confidence.
- Being ready for an interview with a potential employer.

The broader question was whether the program would feel confident sending an applicant to interview with one of its employers.

### English-language testing discussed in the slides

The slides describe English testing for international candidates, with exceptions for:

- Candidates native to a country where the official language is English.
- Candidates who have completed at least one year of higher education in a country where the official language is English.
- Candidates whose previous study was taught entirely in English.

| Test | Overall minimum shown | Section information |
|---|---|---|
| TOEFL iBT | 95 | At least 19 in each section |
| IELTS | 7 | Additional section requirements apply |

This is a summary of the session material, rather than an independent check of current testing and waiver policies. It does not reproduce the complete IELTS section requirements.

# Duke University — Master in Interdisciplinary Data Science (MIDS)

**Fall 2027 application deadline** · January 28, 2027, at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern (10:59:59 p.m. Central). Duke offers one regular-decision round. [Graduate School deadlines](https://gradschool.duke.edu/admissions/application-deadlines/)

## Application materials

- Transcripts, a résumé, a statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation. Duke prefers references who can speak to the applicant’s preparation for graduate study in their major field.
- A 3–5 minute video explaining one project from work, a course, or a personal interest. The intended viewer is familiar with data science and may work outside the field.
- GRE scores are optional. Applicants should check Duke’s English proficiency requirements and exemption criteria.
- Duke welcomes applicants from any major. Quantitative coursework and programming experience help demonstrate readiness. Applicants can also show their preparation through projects or a portfolio. Prior work experience is valued and recent graduates are welcome.

[Application requirements](https://datascience.duke.edu/admissions/requirements/) · [Admissions FAQ](https://datascience.duke.edu/admissions/faq/)

## Curriculum and applied work

MIDS is a two-year, on-campus program. Recent cohorts have had 33–53 students. Students take at least four electives across Duke and can pursue a Quantitative Finance concentration, with courses in time series, derivatives and risk management, and decision optimization in banking. [Quantitative Finance concentration](https://datascience.duke.edu/academics/electives/quantitative-finance/)

| When | What students do |
|---|---|
| Before the first semester | Complete online review modules in linear algebra, statistics, and Python, followed by a three-week, in-person coding bootcamp in August. |
| First year | Study statistical and causal inference, project design, data engineering, machine learning, and natural language processing. |
| Summer between years | Complete a required internship. Students apply to organizations of interest with support from MIDS staff. |
| Second year | Study data ethics and visualization alongside electives. Teams of 3–5 spend September through April on a capstone with a partner organization. Past finance partners include BNY, Citizens Financial Group, and Neuberger Berman. |

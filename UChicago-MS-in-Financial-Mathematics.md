## What the program looks for

- **Strong math:** multivariable calculus, linear algebra and probability are required. The program says it "cannot afford to be flexible" about these.
- **Programming:** some skill required; C++ or Python preferred.
- **Work experience:** not required, but given considerable weight.
- **Excellence in academics and leadership**, from diverse backgrounds.
- **Application:** three statements (250 words or fewer each, including one on your greatest preparation shortcoming), two video statements, and three recommendation letters (at least one professional). GRE optional.

## Where graduates go (December 2025 class)

- **Roles:** quant research 43%, trading 26%, quant development 13%, quant risk 10%
- **Locations:** 78% in the US, led by Chicago (27%) and New York (22%)
- **Employers include:** Citadel, DRW, IMC, Belvedere Trading, Chicago Trading Company, Millennium, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, PIMCO
